    How to play

    1. Read what to wait for.Each round names one thing: a carnivore, a landmark in France, an EGOT winner.
    2. Watch the slides.After a three, two, one, a new slide comes up every two seconds. Nearly all of them are not it.
    3. Hit the button when you see one.The right slide can be the very first, or take half a minute to turn up. First hand on the button while it's showing wins the round.
    4. Quick draw.Hit it inside eight tenths of a second and the round is worth double.
    5. Wrong slide?The button buzzes and the tape says what you hit. You sit out the rest of the round.
    6. It doesn't wait.The right slide stays up exactly as long as the others. If it goes by untapped, nobody scores.
    7. Win.Winners get an emoji. Most points after ten rounds takes it.
